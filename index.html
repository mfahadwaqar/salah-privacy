Salah privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Applies to the Salah app for Android (package app.salah.prayer).

In short: Salah does not collect your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. Everything you set up stays on your phone.

Who we are

Salah is made by Muhammad Fahad Waqar (the developer). You can reach me about this policy or about privacy at islamiverse29@gmail.com.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Approximate location: only when you ask the app to find your city.
  • Notifications: to show prayer reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders and the Adhan arrive on time.
  • Full-screen alerts: optional, so the prayer alert can open over the lock screen.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: so the Adhan can play while the screen is off.
  • Run at startup: so your reminders are set again after the phone restarts.

The app does not request the internet permission, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or your precise (GPS) location.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone, because we do not receive it in the first place. There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services inside the app.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use "Send feedback", the app opens your own email app with your message ready. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. If you send it, I receive the message and your email address as normal email, and I use it only to read and answer your feedback. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Donations

If a donation option is offered, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Salah does not see or store your card or payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to that payment. A donation does not change what the app can do.

Widgets and the Adhan recordings

Home-screen widgets show prayer times from the data on your phone and do not use the internet. The Adhan recordings are stored inside the app.

Children

Salah is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings live only on your phone. Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove them. There is nothing stored for you on any server.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is no data for us to give you, correct or delete. If you have sent me feedback by email and want it deleted, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, for example by adding an internet feature, I will update this page and the date above before the change reaches you.
